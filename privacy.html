Clearly: Checkbook

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Clearly: Checkbook ("Clearly") is made by Jaime Rodriguez. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Clearly does not collect your data.

Information we collect

None. I don't run servers that receive your transactions, accounts, balances, notes or any other content you enter. I can't see them.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone. If you use iCloud sync (part of Plus), your data is also stored in your own private iCloud database so it can appear on your other devices. That database is operated by Apple and is only accessible with your Apple Account. I have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

Clearly contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Features that use your device

  • Face ID or passcode lock: if you turn on the app lock, your device checks your identity. Clearly never receives or stores your biometric data.
  • Reminders: optional bill and plan reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications.
  • CSV import: files you choose to import are read on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

Plus and Pro are one-time purchases handled by the App Store. Apple processes your payment. I don't receive your payment details. Clearly learns only, on your device, which purchases you own.

Children

Clearly is a personal finance tool and is not directed to children. Because Clearly doesn't collect personal information, it holds no children's data either.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove the data stored on your device. To remove data stored in iCloud, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage on your iPhone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
