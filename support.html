Common questions

Does Clearly connect to my bank?

No. You enter your own transactions, or import a CSV file exported from your bank. Clearly never asks for bank logins.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open any locked screen, tap See Plus (or See Pro), then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I sync my checkbooks between devices?

iCloud sync is part of Plus. Sign in to the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud for Clearly on each device. After you unlock Plus, close and reopen Clearly once to start syncing. Your data syncs through your own private iCloud, which only you can access.

What do Plus and Pro include?

Clearly is free with two accounts, unlimited transactions, running balances and cleared or pending tracking. Plus (one-time purchase) adds unlimited accounts, account groups, recurring transactions, custom categories, CSV import, savings plans, full reports and iCloud sync. Pro (one-time purchase) adds the cash-flow forecast, budgets and smart rules. There are no subscriptions.

Can I change the order of my transactions?

Yes. Tap Reorder under the filter in a checkbook, drag a row by its handle and the running balances follow. Moving a row to another day changes its date. An Undo button appears right after a move.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove Clearly's data in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Forecasts in Clearly are estimates based on the transactions and recurring items you've entered. They are not financial advice.
